## The Forest Loner

Frank lives alone in this house, located in a forest in the USA. He’s a proud American, but after his wife left him (and coincidentally also went missing, never to be found again), Frank has been spending too much time alone with his thoughts. But don’t call him insane, actually don’t come near him at all. He wants to be left alone and will shoot anyone that enters his property.
